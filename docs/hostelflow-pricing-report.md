# HostelFlow pricing recommendation — small independent Thai hostel

## Assumptions used

- Pilot property size: **45 beds** (midpoint of the stated 40–50); this is context only, not a source of assumed occupancy or revenue growth.
- Labour is valued at **THB 90 per productive staff-hour**, representing a blended, fully loaded front-desk/admin cost (wages, statutory costs, and paid non-productive time). This should be replaced with the hostel's actual cost.
- Savings count only reduced manual work, avoidable mistakes/leakage, faster reconciliation, and displaced software. **No occupancy, ADR, booking-volume, or revenue-growth benefit is counted.**
- “Staff time saved” covers reservation edits, assignments, check-in/out, transaction look-up, and staff activity follow-up. “Reconciliation/reporting” is separately limited to cash closing, daily reconciliation, and report preparation, so the two are not double-counted.
- Mistake and leakage figures are deliberately modest expected monthly losses avoided—not the value of every error—and assume HostelFlow is used consistently with proper cash-handling controls.
- Software avoidance assumes no full channel manager is replaced. Since HostelFlow has no full channel-manager integration, the model gives it **zero credit for channel-manager replacement or reduced OTA commission**.

## Estimated measurable monthly savings (THB)

| Source of value | Conservative | Realistic | Optimistic | Explicit operating assumption |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---|
| Staff time saved | 1,080 | 1,800 | 2,700 | 12 / 20 / 30 hours saved × THB 90/hour |
| Fewer booking and payment mistakes | 400 | 1,000 | 2,200 | Expected monthly reversals, wrong balances, duplicate/omitted entries, and correction effort avoided |
| Reduced cash and revenue leakage | 500 | 1,200 | 2,600 | Expected monthly unrecorded/misallocated cash and accountability losses avoided through transactions, adjustments, and audit records |
| Faster daily reconciliation and reporting | 540 | 1,080 | 1,800 | 6 / 12 / 20 hours saved × THB 90/hour |
| Avoided other-software cost | 0 | 1,000 | 2,000 | Replacement/consolidation of lightweight PMS, reporting, or record-keeping tools only; no channel-manager credit |
| **Total measurable savings** | **2,520** | **6,080** | **11,300** | |

These are estimates of economic value, not a guarantee. The realistic case equates to **32 saved staff-hours/month**, plus THB 2,200/month in expected error/leakage reduction and THB 1,000/month in avoided tools.

## Price recommendation

Price the standard subscription at **THB 1,290 per hostel per month, excluding VAT**. It captures **21.2%** of the realistic measurable value (THB 1,290 ÷ THB 6,080), inside the requested 15–25% value-capture target and still leaves a meaningful cushion in the conservative case.

- Customer net benefit in the realistic case: **THB 4,790/month** (THB 6,080 − THB 1,290).
- Customer ROI in the realistic case: **371% per month** on subscription spend ((THB 6,080 − THB 1,290) ÷ THB 1,290). Put differently, each THB 1 spent returns about THB 4.71 in net measurable benefit.
- **Recommended pilot price:** **THB 790/month**, for a defined three-month pilot, excluding VAT. It lowers adoption friction while preserving a paid implementation signal; transition to the standard price should be agreed before the pilot begins.
- **Recommended standard monthly price:** **THB 1,290/month**, excluding VAT.
- **Recommended annual prepaid price:** **THB 12,900/year**, excluding VAT (equivalent to ten monthly payments; two months free versus monthly billing).
- **Onboarding fee:** **Not justified for a normal pilot/self-onboarded small hostel.** The product needs easy adoption to prove time and control savings. Charge separately only for clearly scoped exceptional work such as historical-data migration, bespoke configuration, or on-site staff training.

**Final recommended number: THB 1,290 per hostel per month (excluding VAT).**

## Five hostel data points that would most improve this estimate

1. Actual monthly front-desk/admin hours spent on reservations, edits, bed assignment, check-in/out, payment entry, and staff follow-up.
2. Fully loaded hourly cost for each role doing that work, including overtime and employer costs.
3. Average monthly hours spent on daily cash closing, reconciliation, revenue reporting, and investigating discrepancies.
4. Last 6–12 months of quantified booking/payment errors, refunds/adjustments, cash variances, and unexplained revenue discrepancies.
5. Current monthly spend on PMS, reporting/accounting aids, spreadsheets/templates, and any tools HostelFlow can genuinely retire (separating the channel manager, which HostelFlow does not yet replace).
